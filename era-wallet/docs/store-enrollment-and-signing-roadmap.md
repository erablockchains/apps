# ERA Wallet store-enrollment and signing roadmap

`STORE_ENROLLMENT_STATUS: Pending organization verification and D-U-N-S processing`

ERA Projects is awaiting its D-U-N-S number before completing the relevant organization-enrollment and publication processes. No approval date, application number, or store-account identifier is asserted here.

1. Receive the D-U-N-S number.
2. Complete organization verification.
3. Complete Google Play organization enrollment.
4. Confirm the Google Play app-signing strategy.
5. Confirm Android upload/release certificate lineage.
6. Complete Apple Developer organization enrollment.
7. Configure Apple signing and notarization.
8. Complete Microsoft Partner Center organization enrollment.
9. Obtain trusted Windows code-signing capability.
10. Rebuild platform artifacts where required.
11. Recalculate checksums.
12. Complete store-specific QA.
13. Submit to stores.
14. Publish verified store links on `eraprojects.io`.

Google, Apple, and Microsoft have distinct enrollment, signing, review, and packaging requirements. Each store activity requires a separate authorized release review; this direct-download rebuild performs none of them.
