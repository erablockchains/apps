- AI predictions may be incorrect.
- Blockchain transactions are irreversible after finality.
- Payouts and profits are not guaranteed.
- Use only amounts you can afford to lose.
- ETKN value, availability, liquidity, fees, and protocol rules can change.
- Runtime upgrades can temporarily disable wallet signing until compatibility is reviewed.
- Settlement, disputes, eligibility, and claims are determined by chain state and may not match a user's expectation.
- Local devices can be lost, compromised, damaged, or misconfigured.
- Losing a recovery phrase can permanently prevent recovery; exposing it can result in permanent asset loss.
- Endpoint outages, pruned history, network partitions, or ambiguous submissions may delay confirmation. Never retry until chain reconciliation is conclusive.
- Downloaded applications can be impersonated or modified. Use official sources and verify SHA-256 checksums and signatures.
- Availability may be restricted by jurisdiction, rollout stage, compliance decision, or technical readiness.

Nothing in the wallet or its documentation promises profit, prediction accuracy, payout, uninterrupted access, or suitability for a particular purpose.
