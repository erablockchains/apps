## Privacy and store disclosures to reconcile

Local mnemonic/seed, PIN verification material, account selection and preferences remain device data. Public addresses and chain queries, including NFT/reward inspections, go to the configured ERA RPC/metadata service; those providers can observe network metadata. Signed transactions, if the user explicitly submits one, are public chain data. Camera is for QR scanning, biometric/device authentication is mediated by the OS, and the app does not claim first-party cloud backup or telemetry. OS backup or user-selected sharing may still expose exported public reports or local files. The July privacy draft in this directory mentions historical AI tooling; it requires a V14 legal/privacy reconciliation and a live policy URL before store submission. Complete Play Data safety/Financial features/crypto-wallet declarations, Apple App Privacy and export-compliance answers, and Microsoft privacy/support fields from the final binary and service behavior. Do not claim that no data leaves the device.

The live `https://www.eraprojects.io/en/support/` identifies ERA Projects Development LLC and `support@eraprojects.io`. It is a candidate support URL, but its wallet notice still describes the website-published build3 and the then-pending Android device acceptance; update that notice before presenting build4 as a store release. The live `https://www.eraprojects.io/en/privacy-policy/` is a general website policy, not an accepted wallet-specific policy. A wallet-specific V14 disclosure and legal review remain required before submission. Confirm organization name, contact, response route and jurisdictional wording in the actual developer accounts.
