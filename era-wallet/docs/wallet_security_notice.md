# ERA Wallet security notice

ERA Wallet is self-custody software. ERA Projects does not hold your recovery phrase.

1. Write the recovery phrase down offline and verify it before funding the wallet.
2. Never give the phrase, private key, PIN, or password to support or enter it on an unverified website.
3. Use a unique 6–12 digit PIN and enable device authentication.
4. Keep the device, operating system, and wallet release current.
5. Download only from <https://www.eraprojects.io/> or a verified official store listing.
6. Verify the published SHA-256 checksum before installation.
7. Confirm the in-app endpoint, genesis, runtime, and compatibility status before signing.
8. Review recipient, amount, fee, call, signer, and risk warning before approval.
9. Do not retry a transaction whose outcome is uncertain; reconcile finalized chain state first.
10. Treat any exposed recovery phrase as compromised and move assets to a newly generated wallet from a clean device.

Android screenshot protection and best-effort clipboard clearing reduce risk but cannot defend a rooted/jailbroken device, malware, hostile keyboard/accessibility software, external camera, or compromised operating system.
