# ERA Wallet crypto and financial-feature disclosure

**LEGAL_REVIEW: REQUIRED**  
**COMPLIANCE_REVIEW: REQUIRED**

ERA Wallet is non-custodial software that creates/imports user-controlled ERA accounts, reads public chain state, signs transactions locally, transfers native ETKN, performs staking actions, and allows eligible users to participate in AI Prediction YES/NO markets and claim on-chain payouts.

ERA Projects does not receive or custody the user's recovery phrase through the wallet, guarantee token value, promise profit, provide investment advice, or guarantee a prediction outcome or payout. Public addresses and signed transaction data are transmitted to the configured ERA RPC and become visible on-chain.

Before distribution, reviewers must determine applicable wallet, virtual-asset, financial promotion, prediction-market, gaming/gambling, consumer protection, sanctions, age, geographic, tax, and licensing obligations for every target jurisdiction and store.
